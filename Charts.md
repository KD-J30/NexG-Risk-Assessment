# Risk Rating Methodology

# Impact

| Rating | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Low (1)** | Limited effect on operations, information, or resources. |
| **Moderate (2)** | Noticeable effect that may affect business activities. |
| **High (3)** | Significant effect on important information, resources, or operations. |
| **Very High (4)** | Severe effect on critical information, resources, or business operations. |

# Likelihood

| Rating | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Low (1)** | The scenario is unlikely to occur. |
| **Moderate (2)** | The scenario could reasonably occur. |
| **High (3)** | The scenario is likely to occur, or there is significant opportunity for it to occur. |
| **Very High (4)** | The scenario is highly likely to occur. |
